# Dankrupt Exchange: Privacy

Last updated November 26, 2026.

**What the app stores, in Reddit's app storage for the subreddit where you play:**

- your Reddit username, used as your trader name on the standings
- your orders, trades, positions, coin balances, results, streak and notices
- the memes you star, and when you viewed a meme page, counted at most once every five minutes per meme; these counts feed the in-game attention signal

**What the app doesn't do:** collect your email address or any other contact details, show ads, track you outside the app, or sell, share or license your data. It reads nothing from your Reddit account beyond your username.

**Public data:** each meme's fair value uses daily Wikipedia pageview counts from Wikipedia's public API. These are aggregate numbers about articles, not about you.

**What other players see:** your username, rank, return and streak on the standings, plus top-three flair in the subreddit. Balances and positions are never shown to anyone else.

**Retention:** trading history is kept while the game runs, so standings and streaks stay accurate. Price candles are kept for two days and equity snapshots for eight. To have your data removed, ask using the contacts below.
