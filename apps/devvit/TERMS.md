Last updated November 26, 2026.

1. **It's a game.** Dankrupt Exchange is a free game played with play coins. Coins have no money value. You can never buy, sell, transfer or cash them out, and no prize, payment or anything of value depends on your balance.
2. **Not financial advice.** Nothing in the game is investment advice, and meme prices have nothing to do with any real security, currency or company.
3. **Fair play.** Don't use more than one account to manipulate a round, automate trading, or exploit bugs. Moderators may remove a player from the standings or reset their wallet for unfair play. If you find a bug, report it.
4. **Meme listings.** Memes are listed by name for commentary and play. Listings are screened, and moderators can halt any meme. When a meme is halted, every open position settles at its mark price.
5. **Reddit's rules apply.** Use of the app is also governed by Reddit's User Agreement and Content Policy.
6. **Changes.** The game, its rules and these terms can change. Rounds may be paused or reset if something breaks.
