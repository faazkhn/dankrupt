## What to learn from the first post

- **Instagram.** Instagram describes predicting whether a viewer will finish or reshare a Reel. The opening names Doge immediately, and the caption gives people a specific friend to think of. A logo intro would spend the opening seconds before anyone knew the premise. Compare average watch time with the 19.0-second runtime and track shares per account reached. [Instagram ranking explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- **YouTube.** YouTube names the choice to watch, average view duration and percentage viewed among its ranking signals. Check “stayed to watch” for the opening and retention at the order preview for the payoff. If viewers stay for Doge but leave at the ticket, simplify that shot in the next cut. The playbook’s 70% figure is an internal target. [YouTube Shorts discovery](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225)
- **X.** Topic relevance and interactions within a viewer’s network help determine recommendations. Lead with the playable idea and give readers a question they can answer from memory. A feature inventory or signup pitch would give them less to discuss. Count replies that name a meme and explain the call. This first post establishes a baseline. [How X selects posts](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-timeline)
