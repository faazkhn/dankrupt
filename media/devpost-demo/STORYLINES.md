# Dankrupt Exchange · Devpost demo · storylines

The one thing: every day you get 100,000 coins to trade memes whose prices follow the internet, and the bell at midnight UTC decides who won.
Spine: one player (u/faazkhn) takes one meme (Skibidi Toilet) from first tap to the bell.
Assumptions: 45–55 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps. Voice is Faaz's own energetic clone, made locally with Qwen3-TTS from reference-faaz-energetic.wav, at no cost. Burned-in word-by-word captions. Music from the HeyGen catalog, free with your sign-in, plus sound effects. Game screens are captured from the real app build.

## A · "Ten seconds to the bell" (recommended: the daily stakes are the hook, and the hook is the game)
Device: a number lands, a countdown. Form: cut on the beat, with an arcade HUD.
The viewer drops in at 23:59:50 UTC, holding Skibidi Toilet at +31%, with a giant countdown and a "SELL OR HOLD?" prompt. It rewinds to the morning's Market Day post and plays the day in fast arcade cuts. The bell slams and the payoff is the result card: rank #1, a 🥇 flair landing on the username, and a fresh 100k wallet for tomorrow.
First 2 s: the black doodle ground, the 00:00:10 countdown ticking in huge Lilita type, the Skibidi Toilet price flickering green behind it, a heartbeat thump, and the voice: "Ten seconds to the bell."
Beats: countdown hook → rewind ("this morning…") → reveal: logo slam "DANKRUPT EXCHANGE" → proof as verbs: open, pick, buy, watch it move, short the cringe → the bell, the result card and the flair → claim "Fresh coins. Every day." → end card with the logo and "r/dankrupt".
Look: the brand's black doodle ground with neon green and red candles, the cardboard UI punched in at 1.8×, Lilita One. Treatment: a scanline and HUD frame on the countdown only.
Sound: a hard 140 bpm trap or phonk beat, a coin-clink sound effect on fills, a boxing-bell hit on the close, and music ducking under the voice.

## B · "Choose your meme" (the bold one)
Device: a fighting-game trailer. Form: an object-to-object chain.
It's a character-select screen where the "fighters" are memes: Moo Deng, Skibidi Toilet, Chill Guy, Grimace Shake, each with stat bars for HEAT and MOMENTUM taken from the real attention card. PLAYER 1 locks in a meme, and the card zooms into its live chart, which becomes the order ticket, which becomes the standings. "ROUND 1: FIGHT" is the opening bell; "K.O." is the closing bell.
First 2 s: a slam to a roster grid of meme cards, the cursor sweeping across them, and the voice: "Choose your meme."
Beats: roster → lock-in → chart → buy → rival shorts it → bell, K.O. → flair → "New fighter every day" (the daily listing) → end card.
Look: a versus-screen split of green against red, chunky arcade type, real UI inside the cards.
Sound: an arcade announcer energy in your voice, a chiptune and trap hybrid, and hit sound effects on every cut.

## C · "5:30 am"
Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: one continuous take.
It's 5:30 in the morning in India, which is midnight UTC: the bell. A phone lights up, Reddit is open, the new Market Day #2 thread is at the top of the feed, and the camera never cuts as it scrolls in, taps, sees yesterday's result card, and trades today's new listing.
First 2 s: the 5:30 lock-screen clock, then the voice: "The bell just rang."
Beats: alarm → feed → result card → today's listing → trade → share the call → claim → end card.
Look: a phone in hand on the brand ground, with the real Reddit feed. Sound: lo-fi that builds into the beat.
