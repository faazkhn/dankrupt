# 23: Daily "Market Close" video (post-alpha)

**What to build:** Every day at market close (midnight IST), generate a short video from engine data for social posting. It covers the day's top gainers and losers, the best "I called it" trade, the top 3 on the daily board, new IPOs and comebacks. It is rendered with HyperFrames, and posting is manual.

**Blocked by:** 14

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Format to confirm with Faaz:** vertical 9:16, 30 to 45 seconds, no voiceover, burned-in captions. The output is an MP4 file the operator downloads.

- [ ] A daily job pulls the day's numbers from projections and renders the video without manual steps.
- [ ] It uses no re-hosted meme images: names, prices, charts and handles only.
- [ ] Players appear only by public handle.
- [ ] The operator can download the latest video and re-render any past day.
